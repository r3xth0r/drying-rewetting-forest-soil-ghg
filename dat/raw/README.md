# Raw Datasets

Filenames use lowercase kebab-case. CSV files contain flux, soil, or water-input measurements; NetCDF files contain annual SPEI data.

- `ch4-klausen.csv`: CH4 fluxes at KL after QC in µg CH4-C m-2 h-1
- `ch4-zoe.csv`: CH4 fluxes at ZB after QC in µg CH4-C m-2 h-1
- `co2-klausen.csv`: CO2 fluxes at KL after QC in mg CO2-C m-2 h-1
- `co2-zoe.csv`: CO2 fluxes at ZB after QC in mg CO2-C m-2 h-1
- `irrigation-klausen.csv`: irrigation events with date and irrigation in mm at KL
- `irrigation-zb.csv`: irrigation events with date and irrigation in mm at ZB
- `n2o-klausen.csv`: N2O fluxes at KL after QC in µg N2O-N m-2 h-1
- `precipitation-klausen.csv`: precipitation events with raw data and precipitation in mm at KL
- `precipitation-zb.csv`: precipitation events with raw data and precipitation in mm at ZB
- `soil-moisture-klausen.csv`: volumetric soil moisture (%) at KL at 5, 15, and 30 cm
- `soil-moisture-zb.csv`: volumetric soil moisture (%) at ZB at 10 cm
- `soil-depth-sampling.csv`: soil carbon, nitrogen, and water measurements from two-depth sampling campaigns
- `soil-parameter-survey.csv`: soil carbon, nitrogen, and water measurements across sites and sampling events
- `soil-temperature-klausen.csv`: soil temperature (°C) at KL at 5, 15, and 30 cm
- `soil-temperature-zb.csv`: soil temperature (°C) at ZB at 10 cm
- `winfore2-spei30-<YYYY>.nc`: gridded SPEI30 drought index values (dimensionless) for each year from 2016–2024, used to extract May–October values at KL and ZB
